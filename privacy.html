Meadow Money — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Meadow Money is a personal savings and income tracker. This policy explains what the app accesses and why.

Two ways to use Meadow Money

On this device only (“Start now”). No sign-in is needed. Your income, expenses, goals, settings and receipt photos are stored in this browser's local storage (localStorage and IndexedDB) on this device. Nothing is sent to Google, the developer or anyone else. If you clear this site's data, uninstall the app or change devices, those records are gone unless you have backed them up.

With Google Drive backup (optional). If you sign in with Google, your records are also saved to your own Google Drive as described below, so you can use them on other devices.

What the app accesses

If you choose to sign in with Google, Meadow Money asks for two permissions: your email address (to show which account you are signed in with), and access only to files the app itself creates in your Google Drive (the drive.file permission). It cannot see, read or change any of your other Drive files.

Where your data is stored

Everything you enter — income, expenses, savings goals, settings and receipt photos — is saved in your own Google Drive, in a spreadsheet named "Meadow Money Data" and a folder named "Meadow Money Receipts". The app has no server and no database of its own. The developer cannot see your data.

Receipt scanning

Receipt photos are uploaded to your own Drive. To read the total, the app uses Google Drive's built-in text recognition by creating a temporary document in your Drive, reading its text, and then deleting it.

Sharing

Your data is never sold, shared, or sent anywhere other than your own Google account. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

On this device, clear this site's data in your browser settings. In Drive, delete the "Meadow Money Data" sheet and "Meadow Money Receipts" folder from your Drive at any time. You can remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
